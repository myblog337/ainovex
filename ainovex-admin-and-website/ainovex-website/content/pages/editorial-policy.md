## Our standards

- We do not invent pricing, features, ratings, statistics or test results. If we cannot verify something, we say so.
- Guides state the date they were last reviewed. Pricing and limits change often, so confirm details on the vendor's website before buying.
- Where we tested a tool ourselves, we describe what we did. Where we did not, we say the description is based on the vendor's documentation.

## Independence

Commercial relationships never decide rankings or conclusions. Sponsored content, if we publish any, is clearly labelled.

## Use of AI in our work

AI tools may assist with research or drafting, but a person reviews and is accountable for everything we publish.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, [contact us](/contact/). We update the guide and its "Updated" date.
