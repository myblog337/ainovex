## Information we collect

The site has no user accounts or comments. AINOVEX may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage and improve content. The Google Analytics measurement ID is configured separately and is not published in this policy. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard server logs such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. Site search runs in your browser, but the search term appears in the page address and may therefore appear in those logs.

## Cookies and advertising

AINOVEX does not currently use Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Where required (including the UK, EU and Canada) we will ask for consent first. You can manage ad personalisation at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
